New

Port forwarding, built in

Reach a service on any of your devices — SSH, a remote desktop, a dev server, a router's admin page — as if it were local. Connections tunnel over the same encrypted sync link, so there's nothing extra to expose or configure.

  • ✓ Map In (a device + port) to Out (another device + host + port), with a note per rule.
  • ✓ Listen on loopback only, or open a port to your whole LAN with one toggle.
  • ✓ A live status light shows every forward — listening, failed, or offline — at a glance.
